FAQ

FAQ

What do you need from us to get started?

The cities you need to connect in China and overseas, what the connection is for (such as overseas SaaS, or links to an overseas HQ or data center), and your bandwidth and latency needs.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the line type and the cities at each end; your proposal will give a firm timeline.

What is the difference between IPLC and IEPL?

Both are dedicated point-to-point international lines. IPLC is a traditional leased circuit; IEPL is an international Ethernet private line that connects over Ethernet ports at both ends, which makes networking more flexible. We choose based on your equipment and needs at each end.

International private line or global application acceleration?

Fixed sites such as an overseas HQ or data center usually use an international private line with dedicated bandwidth. For staff using overseas SaaS and business systems, global application acceleration improves speed and stability. The two can also be combined.

Can we test it first?

Yes. Once the design is agreed, we arrange a free trial.
