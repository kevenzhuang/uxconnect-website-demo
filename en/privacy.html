Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This policy applies to the official website operated by Shanghai Youxun Industrial Co., Ltd. (uXconnect, "we" or "us"), registered at Room 404, 4F, Building D3, 39 Hongcao Road, Xuhui District, Shanghai, China. If you have any questions, please use the contact details at the end of this page.

What we collect

We only collect personal information when you submit the consultation form:

  • Company name, your name and phone number (required)
  • Email, WeChat ID, office address or building, and your requirements (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • The time of submission and your IP address, used only to prevent spam and abuse

You can browse this website without providing any personal information.

How we use it

We use this information only to contact you, understand your needs, prepare a solution and quote, and arrange a pilot and follow-up service. We do not use it for unrelated marketing, and we never sell it.

Storage and transfer

Form submissions are stored on our servers in mainland China, and the colleague following up is notified through WeCom or company email. Only staff working on your inquiry can see them. If you submit the form from outside mainland China, your information will be transferred to and stored in mainland China.

We keep your information for as long as needed for the inquiry and any resulting business, and no longer than 3 years from submission. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except: with your explicit consent; where partners such as telecom carriers need limited information to deliver a service to you (we require them to protect it too); or where required by law or regulators.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies, third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and does not load third-party fonts or scripts.

Your rights

You can access, correct, copy or delete the personal information you submitted, and withdraw your consent. Email youxunxingzheng@youxun.com and we will respond within 15 business days.

Children

This website is intended for business customers and is not directed at children under 14.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business or the law changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.
