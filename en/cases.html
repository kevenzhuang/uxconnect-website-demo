ManufacturingA European automotive parts groupAutomotive · Cross-Border Networks
1 business dayRouting changes

The challenge

  • Its China headquarters in Shanghai needed stable, low-latency access from its data center to Hong Kong and overseas
  • Routing had to be adjustable quickly whenever latency or packet loss rose
  • It wanted clear usage billing and network quality reporting

Our solution

  • Our own metro network links the customer's data center directly to our Shanghai PoP, aggregating via domestic nodes to our Hong Kong egress
  • The customer's IP space is announced through multiple upstreams including NTT, PCCW, HKIX and Equinix, with automatic best-path selection
  • A dedicated European route is planned over subsea cable to a data center in Amsterdam

The result

Routing issues are adjusted within one business day, with monthly latency and packet-loss reports, a ticketing system and a 24/7 hotline.
