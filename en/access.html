FAQ

FAQ

How do I know if my building is covered?

Send us the building name or address. A solutions consultant will reply within one business day with coverage and an installation plan.

How long does installation take?

Covered buildings can be connected quickly. Exact timing depends on the site survey, and we align it with your move-in schedule.

Is there a bandwidth limit? Can I change it later?

Bandwidth is sized to your needs with no upper limit, and you can upgrade at any time.

Do you provide static public IPs?

Yes.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Our hotline takes requests 24/7, and engineers can be on site within two hours (force majeure excepted). A four-level escalation path steps up at 4, 8 and 12 hours, up to the general manager.

Can we test it first?

Yes. Office connectivity comes with a 14-day free pilot, no strings attached.
