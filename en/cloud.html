Services

Cloud and data center services

Business traffic stays off the public internet, running over our backbone straight to cloud and data center.

Cloud connect

Direct links from your site to AWS, Azure, Alibaba Cloud and other public clouds over our backbone, bypassing internet congestion.

Data center interconnect

Layer 2 point-to-point and meshed links between major data centers in China.

Hybrid and multi-cloud

Direct links between your own data center and public clouds, or between clouds, with backup on every path.

Data center sourcing

Racks, bandwidth and cloud on-ramps sourced from carrier and third-party data centers, delivered as one service.
