Enterprise networking services

Every design is tailored to your sites, criticality and budget, and usually mixes approaches: dedicated lines for core sites, SD-WAN for branches.

SD-WAN

A WAN built on internet links, at lower cost than traditional private lines. Centrally managed, live as soon as the device arrives.

MPLS-VPN

A Layer 3 private network on carrier backbones, well isolated and stable, in hub-and-spoke, mesh or tiered designs.

Layer 2 networks

VPLS and VXLAN over EVPN, so every site sits on the same LAN.

Domestic point-to-point lines

Dedicated Ethernet and MSTP circuits, sized to need with no upper limit, typically live in 10 to 20 days.

FAQ

FAQ

What do you need from us to get started?

The number of sites and their cities, the lines each site uses today, your main business systems (such as ERP, CRM and VoIP) and how critical they are. We design the network from there.

How long does installation take?

SD-WAN devices come online as soon as they are plugged in on site, so timing mostly depends on shipping. Domestic point-to-point lines usually take 10 to 20 days.

Should we choose SD-WAN or dedicated lines?

SD-WAN runs over internet links and costs less than traditional private lines, which suits stores and branches. MPLS-VPN and point-to-point lines offer strong isolation and steady quality for core sites. Most businesses combine them: dedicated lines for core sites, SD-WAN for branches.

Do we need to replace our existing routers and firewalls?

Not necessarily. Our CPE supports routed, bridged, bypass and inline deployment, so we can fit it to your current network. The exact setup is settled during design.

Can we test it first?

Yes. We can deploy test devices free of charge so you can check performance with real business traffic.
