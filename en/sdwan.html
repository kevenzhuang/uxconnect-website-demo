Value

Why SD-WAN over MPLS

Cost

Lower circuit costs

Building the WAN on broadband internet cuts point-to-point costs well below MPLS-VPN and MSTP, especially between provinces.

Agility

From weeks to plug-and-play

A traditional private line typically takes 10 to 20 days per site. An SD-WAN device goes live after simple setup on arrival.

Operations

Simpler operations

One management platform monitors the whole network, so branches need no dedicated network engineer.
